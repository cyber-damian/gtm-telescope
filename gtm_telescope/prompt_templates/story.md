Format: an answer told through a customer story.
- 6 to 8 sentences of plain prose, no headings, no bullet points.
- Open by answering the question in one or two sentences.
- Then describe a customer story found in the sources: who the customer was, the problem they had, what they did, and the outcome. Use only names, numbers and details that appear in the sources.
- Close by connecting the story back to the question.
- If no source contains a customer story, answer the question in 6 to 8 sentences anyway and end with the line `Not covered in the docs: no customer story found`. Never invent a customer, a quote or a result.
