# Platform pricing and limits

## Starter tier

Starter includes 25 seats and a rate-limit of 600 API requests per minute per workspace. Overage requests are throttled, not billed.

## Enterprise tier

Enterprise includes unlimited seats, a negotiated API rate-limit, and a 99.9% uptime SLA. Pricing is annual and quoted per workspace.
