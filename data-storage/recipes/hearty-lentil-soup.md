# Hearty lentil soup

A filling vegetarian dinner with legumes and warm spices. Serves four.

## Ingredients

- Red or brown lentils, rinsed
- Onion, carrots, celery
- Garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, bay leaf
- Vegetable stock and a can of diced tomatoes
- Olive oil, salt, pepper, lemon wedges to finish

## Steps

Sweat the mirepoix in olive oil until soft. Add spices and garlic until fragrant. Stir in lentils, tomatoes, and stock; simmer until lentils are tender but not mushy. Adjust seasoning and serve with crusty bread.

## Notes

For extra depth, stir in a spoonful of harissa or a splash of sherry vinegar before serving.
